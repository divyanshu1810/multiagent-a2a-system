# ABC Consultants Ltd. – Return & Refund Policy

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Approved By:** Operations & Executive Leadership  
**Applies To:** All customer purchases via ABC Consultants Ltd.'s platforms

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the conditions, timelines, and procedures for product returns, exchanges, and refunds to ensure a fair and transparent post-purchase experience.

---

## 2. Return Window by Category

| Product Category | Return Window | Condition |
|-----------------|--------------|-----------|
| Electronics | 7 days | Unused, original packaging, all accessories included |
| Home Appliances | 7 days | Unused, original packaging |
| Menswear | 15 days | Unworn, tags attached, original packaging |
| Womenswear | 15 days | Unworn, tags attached, original packaging |
| Home Décor | 10 days | Unused, original packaging |

> **Note:** Return window begins from the date of delivery confirmation.

---

## 3. Eligible Return Reasons

Returns are accepted for the following reasons:

1. **Defective or damaged product** – Item received with manufacturing defect or physical damage
2. **Wrong item delivered** – Item received differs from what was ordered
3. **Size/fit issue** (Apparel only) – Wrong size delivered or size does not match description
4. **Missing parts or accessories** – Incomplete product
5. **Product not as described** – Significant variation from listing description
6. **Dead on arrival (DOA)** – Electronics/appliances non-functional upon first use

---

## 4. Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned:

- Items damaged due to customer misuse, improper handling, or accident
- Products with tampered serial numbers or removed warranty stickers
- Customized, personalized, or made-to-order items
- Items marked "Non-Returnable" on the product listing
- Intimate apparel, innerwear, and swimwear (hygiene reasons)
- Software, digital downloads, or licensed media

---

## 5. Return Process

### Step 1: Initiate Return
- Log in to account → Orders → Select Order → "Return/Exchange"
- Select reason and upload supporting photos (mandatory for damaged/wrong items)
- Submit return request within the eligible return window

### Step 2: Return Approval
- Return request reviewed within **24–48 business hours**
- Approved returns receive a Return Authorization Number (RAN)
- Declined returns are communicated with reason; escalation option provided

### Step 3: Product Pickup
- For approved returns, reverse pickup scheduled within **2 business days**
- Customer must ensure product is packed in original/adequate packaging
- Pickup agent verifies product condition at the time of collection
- Self-ship option available for locations where pickup is not serviceable; shipping cost reimbursed up to ₹150

### Step 4: Quality Check
- Returned product undergoes quality inspection at our warehouse
- If product passes inspection → refund or exchange initiated
- If product fails inspection (tampered, used, damaged beyond claim) → product returned to customer; return rejected

### Step 5: Refund / Exchange
- **Refund:** Processed within 3–5 business days post quality check approval
- **Exchange:** Replacement dispatched within 2–4 business days post quality check

---

## 6. Exchange Policy

- One exchange per order is permitted within the return window
- Exchange for same product in different size/colour only (category: Apparel)
- For Electronics/Appliances: Exchange for same model only (subject to availability)
- If desired exchange item is out of stock, refund is processed automatically

---

## 7. Refund Modes

| Original Payment | Refund Mode | Timeline |
|-----------------|------------|---------|
| UPI / Net Banking | Source account | 3–5 business days |
| Credit/Debit Card | Source card | 5–7 business days |
| Wallet | Source wallet | 2–3 business days |
| BNPL | Partner credit reversal | As per partner |
| COD | NEFT to registered bank account | 7–10 business days |
| Store Credit | ABC Wallet (instant upon approval) | Same day |

> Customers may opt for **Store Credit** instead of a source refund for faster processing.

---

## 8. Damaged in Transit

- Report damage within **24 hours** of delivery with unboxing video/photos
- No return window restriction for transit damage claims
- Replacement or full refund processed within 48 hours of verification

---

## 9. Warranty Claims vs. Returns

- **Within return window:** Defective products handled via return policy
- **Post return window but within warranty:** Contact brand warranty service center
- ABC Consultants provides facilitation support for warranty claims on registered products

---

## 10. Escalation & Disputes

- Unresolved return disputes: escalate via **helpdesk@abcconsultants.in**
- Consumer disputes may be referred to the appropriate Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum
- Response SLA for escalated disputes: **3 business days**
