# ABC Consultants Ltd. – Delivery Modes & Logistics Partners Policy

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Approved By:** Supply Chain & Executive Leadership  
**Applies To:** All orders fulfilled through ABC Consultants Ltd.'s platforms

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the available delivery modes, logistics partners, service-level agreements (SLAs), and associated terms for order fulfilment across all product categories and geographies served by ABC Consultants Ltd.

---

## 2. Delivery Modes

### 2.1 Standard Delivery
- **Description:** Regular ground/air-surface shipping to the customer's address
- **Delivery Timeline:** 4–7 business days (metro); 6–10 business days (Tier 2/3 cities)
- **Cost:** ₹49 flat fee; free for orders above ₹999
- **Tracking:** End-to-end tracking via partner app or ABC portal
- **Suitable for:** All non-urgent, non-perishable orders

### 2.2 Express Delivery
- **Description:** Priority processing and faster shipment
- **Delivery Timeline:** 1–2 business days (metro & select Tier 2 cities)
- **Cost:** ₹149 flat fee; free for orders above ₹5,000 (select categories)
- **Availability:** PIN-code dependent; shown at checkout if available
- **Suitable for:** Apparel, Electronics accessories, gifts

### 2.3 Same-Day Delivery
- **Description:** Delivered within the same calendar day if ordered before 12:00 PM IST
- **Delivery Timeline:** By 8:00 PM on order day
- **Cost:** ₹299 flat fee (non-waivable)
- **Availability:** Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune only
- **Suitable for:** In-stock items fulfilled from local dark stores

### 2.4 Scheduled Delivery
- **Description:** Customer selects preferred delivery date and time slot
- **Delivery Timeline:** Customer's chosen slot (up to 7 days ahead)
- **Cost:** ₹99 flat fee
- **Time Slots:** 9am–12pm | 12pm–3pm | 3pm–6pm | 6pm–9pm
- **Suitable for:** Large appliances, furniture, bulk orders

### 2.5 Click & Collect (Self Pick-Up)
- **Description:** Customer collects from designated ABC collection point or partner store
- **Ready for Pick-Up:** Within 2 business days of order confirmation
- **Cost:** Free
- **Collection Points:** Available in 50+ cities (select at checkout)
- **Hold Period:** Items held for 5 business days; auto-cancelled if not collected

### 2.6 B2B / Bulk Delivery
- **Description:** Dedicated logistics for corporate and institutional buyers
- **Delivery Timeline:** As per purchase order agreement (typically 7–14 business days)
- **Cost:** Included in B2B contract pricing or quoted separately
- **Features:** Palletized delivery, dock-to-dock, scheduled bulk drops
- **Minimum Order:** ₹1,00,000 or 50 units

## 4. International Shipping

- **Available Destinations:** UAE, USA, UK, Canada, Singapore, Australia (select SKUs only)
- **Carrier:** DHL Express / FedEx International
- **Timeline:** 7–14 business days (subject to customs)
- **Cost:** Calculated at checkout based on weight, dimensions, and destination
- **Customs & Duties:** Payable by recipient as per destination country regulations
- **Restricted Items:** Electronics with lithium batteries, apparel with specific fabrics—subject to export control

---

## 5. Delivery SLAs & Guarantees

| Mode | SLA | Guarantee |
|------|-----|-----------|
| Standard | 4–10 business days | Compensation credit of ₹50 for delays beyond SLA |
| Express | 1–2 business days | Full delivery fee refund for SLA breach |
| Same-Day | Same calendar day | Full delivery fee refund for SLA breach |
| Scheduled | Customer-chosen slot | ₹100 credit for missed slots |
| B2B | Per contract | As per contract SLA penalties |

> SLA clock starts from order confirmation, not payment. Delays due to natural calamities, government restrictions, or force majeure are excluded.

---

## 6. Delivery Instructions & Special Handling

- **Address Accuracy:** Customer responsible for accurate delivery address; re-delivery charges apply for failed delivery due to incorrect address
- **OTP Delivery:** Orders above ₹10,000 require OTP verification at delivery
- **Large Items:** Scheduled delivery mandatory for items >20kg or >5 cubic feet; ground-floor delivery standard; staircase/installation at additional charge
- **Fragile Items:** Double-boxed with fragile markings; "Do Not Stack" label applied

---

## 7. Undeliverable Shipments

- **Reason Codes:** Address not found, customer unavailable after 3 attempts, refused delivery
- **Hold Period:** 5 business days at partner facility before return to origin
- **Redelivery:** Customer may request redelivery within hold period (₹49 charge)
- **Return to Origin (RTO):** Order refunded after deducting forward shipping cost (₹49–₹149)

---

## 8. Tracking & Notifications

- Real-time tracking via SMS, email, and ABC app push notifications
- Tracking link shared upon shipment dispatch
- Estimated Delivery Date (EDD) displayed at order confirmation and updated dynamically
- Proof of Delivery (POD): Digital signature or photo confirmation
