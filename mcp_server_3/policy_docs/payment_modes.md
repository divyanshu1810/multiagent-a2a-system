# ABC Consultants Ltd. – Mode of Payment Policy

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Approved By:** Finance & Executive Leadership  
**Applies To:** All e-commerce and B2B transactions on ABC Consultants' platforms

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the accepted modes of payment for all purchases made through ABC Consultants Ltd.'s online and offline platforms, including the terms, conditions, and security standards associated with each payment method.

---

## 2. Accepted Payment Modes

### 2.1 Credit & Debit Cards
- **Accepted Networks:** Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express
- **Currencies:** INR (domestic); USD, EUR, GBP (international with applicable forex charges)
- **3D Secure:** Mandatory for all card transactions above ₹2,000
- **EMI Options:** Available on select banks for orders above ₹5,000; 3, 6, 9, 12-month tenures
- **Surcharge:** No surcharge for domestic cards; 1.5% international card surcharge applies

### 2.2 Net Banking
- **Supported Banks:** SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Yes Bank, Canara Bank, and 40+ others
- **Transaction Limit:** As per individual bank limits (typically ₹5 lakh per transaction)
- **Processing Time:** Real-time for most banks; up to 4 hours for some cooperative banks

### 2.3 UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
- **Supported Apps:** Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay, and all UPI-enabled apps
- **Transaction Limit:** ₹1 lakh per transaction (NPCI mandated); ₹2 lakh for verified merchants
- **Processing Time:** Instant
- **Preferred Method:** UPI is the preferred payment mode due to zero MDR

### 2.4 Wallets
- **Accepted Wallets:** Paytm, Mobikwik, Freecharge, Amazon Pay, PhonePe wallet
- **Maximum Balance Usage:** As per RBI KYC norms (₹10,000 for minimum KYC; ₹2 lakh for full KYC)
- **Refunds:** Credited back to source wallet within 2–5 business days

### 2.5 Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)
- **Partners:** ZestMoney, Simpl, LazyPay, Flipkart Pay Later, Amazon Pay Later
- **Eligibility:** Subject to partner credit assessment
- **Interest:** Zero-interest for 15-day payment period; 1.5–3% monthly interest post period
- **Maximum Credit:** Up to ₹50,000 based on partner assessment

### 2.6 Cash on Delivery (COD)
- **Availability:** Serviceable PIN codes only (check at checkout)
- **Order Limit:** Maximum ₹25,000 per order
- **Handling Fee:** ₹50 COD handling charge applies
- **Restriction:** Not available for items marked "Prepaid Only" or perishable/customized goods

### 2.7 Corporate / Purchase Orders (B2B)
- **Eligible Clients:** Registered corporate accounts with credit limit pre-approval
- **Credit Terms:** Net 30, Net 45, or Net 60 based on client agreement
- **Invoice Currency:** INR or as per contract
- **Minimum Order Value:** ₹1,00,000 for PO-based transactions

---

## 3. Payment Security Standards

- All transactions processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateway
- Card data is never stored on ABC Consultants' servers (tokenization enforced)
- All payment pages served over HTTPS with TLS 1.2+
- Fraud monitoring: Real-time AI-driven transaction screening
- Suspicious transactions may be flagged for manual review; customer will be notified

---

## 4. Payment Failure & Retry

- On payment failure, amount is auto-refunded within 5–7 business days
- Customers may retry payment up to 3 times within 24 hours for the same order
- If payment debited but order not confirmed, lodge complaint via support within 48 hours

---

## 5. Refunds

| Payment Mode | Refund Timeline |

| Wallet | 1–2 business days |
